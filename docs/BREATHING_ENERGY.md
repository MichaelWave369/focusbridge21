# Breathing Energy | FocusBridge21 Chapter 2, third rung

## Primary source and what it actually says

Source: *The Book of Knowledge*, credited to **Anonymous**. See the **"Breathing Energy"** subsection after "Going Deeper Meditation" in Chapter 2, page 3 of the **112-page PDF** provided by the user.

In the book's three steps:

1. The author requires **Quiet-Mind Meditation (Q-MM) and Going Deeper Meditation (GDM)** first.
2. The author calls for deep breathing in through the nose, breathing out through a small pursed-lip opening, and imagining the body as a **sponge surrounded by golden-white light**.
3. The author says the practitioner must feel the imagined energy, not merely see it.

These are **claims and instructions from the source**, not scientifically verified evidence that energy can enter the body through visualization. The book also attributes extraordinary abilities to later practices. No FocusBridge21 session is designed to validate those claims.

Primary linked edition: https://www.scribd.com/doc/120678976/The-Book-Of-Knowledge

## Our adaptation, and where it differs

The app preserves the imaginative core of this subsection and its prerequisite order. However, it **deliberately does not require deep or forceful inhalation, pursed-lip exhalation, breath holding, a fixed breath ratio, or a certain bodily sensation**. The user may breathe comfortably through the nose or mouth without counting. This change avoids turning an exploratory exercise into a potentially uncomfortable breathing challenge.

The user can select:
- **Golden-white glow** (default), a gentle version of the source's sponge/light image.
- **Soft white light**, a simpler visualization.
- **No-light body awareness**, a completely nonvisual alternative focused on ordinary breathing sensations.

It is entirely okay to notice nothing unusual.

## Stage engine

`docs/fb21-breathing-energy.js` defines six original, non-verbatim stages. Each can be used in an optional 3-, 5-, 10-, 15-, 20- or 30-minute session.

| Stage | Duration share | Focus |
|---|---:|---|
| Supported and present | 14% | Physical comfort and surroundings |
| Comfortable breath | 20% | Natural breathing without force or rhythm counting |
| Sponge and light imagery | 22% | Optional symbolic golden-white light, white light or no visual |
| Notice sensations | 18% | Awareness of actual physical sensations without attributing a cause |
| Quietly integrate | 16% | Rest without needing any special outcome |
| Grounded return | 10% | Hands, feet, eyes and orientation to the room |

The active stage is displayed during the timer. Optional original browser voice cues are off by default on **every** new session. No generated audio is needed, and no headphones are required. The same clear **Return now**, Pause/Resume and safety confirmation from Quiet Deck apply.

## QMM-first journey routing

From the Quiet Deck home screen:

- **Breathing Energy:** QMM → GDM → Breathing Energy.
- **Threshold Explorer or Focus Sync:** QMM → GDM → Breathing Energy → chosen exploration.
- **Standalone QMM:** may offer GDM after a completed timed session.
- **Standalone GDM:** may offer Breathing Energy after its own completed timed session.
- **Standalone Breathing Energy:** may offer Threshold Explorer after a completed session.

Every stage has its **own new safety confirmation** and an entirely **voluntary Continue** button after a completed timed practice and grounded return. An early Return Now **never unlocks** the next stage. A completion receipt says only that the chosen **timer and original guidance sequence** finished. It does **not** claim a quiet mind was attained, a brain frequency changed, or energy entered the body.

Ordinary soundscape listening, work-focus audio, sleep ambience, intention scripts, comfort audio and waveform labs remain outside this chain. The older full laboratory continues to use its own routes and is not retroactively gated.

## Ledger, consent and limits

The local Signal Ledger records selected imagery, final stage, elapsed time, whether speech was opted into, whether the timed session completed, and the intended next exercise (if any). No private imagery, biometric energy readout, or fabricated coherence number is stored.

Meditation and imagery can provoke discomfort for some people. If dizzy, breathless, anxious or disoriented, stop; open your eyes and notice your surroundings. Nothing here is a medical intervention, spiritual certification or measured external phenomenon.
