# Chapter 6: Controlling Energy | FocusBridge21

## Primary source

*The Book of Knowledge*, Anonymous, Chapter 6, **PDF pages 17–18** in the user's supplied 112-page edition (https://www.scribd.com/doc/120678976/The-Book-Of-Knowledge).

The author divides this chapter into several exercises and assertions. **Quiet-Mind Meditation (QMM)** is explicitly the prerequisite for the initial mental-body exercise and the forest exercise; the chapter does not require GDM or Breathing Energy as prerequisites.

| In the author's book | What we implemented |
|---|---|
| Picture a second/mental body standing in front of the physical body, surrounded by warm light; imagine rejoining the physical body | **Mental-body and limbs**: a voluntary, imaginative image, with the body physically remaining supported |
| Project and draw energy through hands and feet | An inward/outward imagined glow at each hand and foot, without claims of real emitted energy |
| Stand barefoot on soil/grass, feel vibrations, picture warm red pulsing plasma from Earth | **Earth connection imagery**: imagine red warmth while seated or supported. No need to go barefoot, stand, or go outside |
| Put hands in real water and send energy right-to-left and back, with assertions about moving/freezing water mentally | **Imagined water exchange**: a wholly dry, mental exercise of alternating ripples between imagined hands |
| Picture an exotic forest, imagine multiple senses, and draw "life-force" from trees and animals | **Forest and living-things imagery**: original, optional sensory descriptions and a gesture of care, with no taking or measuring life-force |
| Detect people's approaching presence, see auras, know their thoughts or futures, scan life on other planets | These are **unverified assertions in the source**, not capabilities we implement, measure, or promise |

The original page 18 ends with a diagram that is not reused here. Some images in the book have uncertain third-party copyright provenance.

## User experience

**Quiet Deck → Controlling Energy · Imagery Studio → QMM → chosen Chapter 6 imagery.**

Users choose **one of four thematic paths** in the Chapter 6 preparation screen:

1. Mental-body and limbs (7 timed stages)
2. Earth connection imagery (5 stages)
3. Imagined water exchange (6 stages)
4. Forest and living-things imagery (6 stages)

Each path contains its own opening orientation and an explicit return to the ordinary room. All prompts are **original and non-verbatim** and are defined in `docs/fb21-chapter6-energy.js`.

Session lengths: **3, 5, 10, 15, 20, or 30 minutes**. No special waveform, soundscape, headphones, audio stimulation, sleep mode or physical activity is needed. Optional browser narration is off by default and must be re-enabled per session. The existing Pause, Resume, Return Now, safety confirmation and local Signal Ledger features remain active.

A QMM session completed to its chosen duration can voluntarily hand off to the Chapter 6 setup after a grounded return and reflection. **Early QMM return never unlocks the chapter.** Chapter 6 also allows early Return Now and accurately records that the timed practice was interrupted.

## Safety and evidence boundaries

- Do not interpret visualizations as proof of telekinesis, thought reading, water manipulation, energy transfer, prediction, paranormal phenomena, life-force detection or spiritual mastery.
- **The water theme requires no actual water.** Do not immerse hands or use electronics near water for this session.
- **The Earth theme requires no barefoot exposure.** Staying safely seated indoors is fine; do not force sensations or environmental exposure.
- No user is asked to physically move, lean, separate from their body, or handle substances. If eyes-closed imagery feels disorienting, keep eyes open, skip the images or use Return Now.
- Nature imagery is a self-generated mental scene. It cannot establish the presence, emotions, thoughts, health status or consent of a real human or animal.
- Local Signal Ledger metadata records theme, duration, last stage, completion of the *timer*, and optional narration. It does not claim any physiological, psychic or physical outcome.
- Preserve the existing independent soundscape/audio practices; Chapter 6 is entirely silent-first.
