# Chapter 7: Cleansing | Seven-Center Symbolic Imagery

## Source, exact order, and interpretation

This is an original guided adaptation of **Chapter 7, “Cleansing,” PDF pages 19–21** of the user's 112-page edition of *The Book of Knowledge* (Anonymous).

The author begins by explicitly requiring **Quiet-Mind Meditation (QMM)**. Chapter 7 then presents a **bottom-up Root-to-Crown** sequence. This **differs from Chapter 4's Crown-to-Root light meditation**. These are separate practices in FocusBridge21, not one reordered or silently reconciled technique.

| Chapter 7 center | Color specified by author | Points described | PDF page |
|---|---|---|---|
| Root | Red | One, at base of spine | 19 |
| Sacral | Orange | Front and back of pelvis | 19 |
| Solar Plexus | Yellow | Front and back of upper abdomen | 20 |
| Heart | Green | Front and back of chest | 20 |
| Throat | Light blue, becoming deeper blue | Front and back of throat | 21 |
| Third Eye | Light purple | Front and imagined rear point | 21 |
| Crown | Golden-white | One, above the head | 21 |

The source says to imagine the light intensifying, feel heat/pulsation, burn away negativity and leave a clean, glowing orb. It also asserts that chakra damage causes physical or mental illness and that sufficiently powerful meditation can cure serious diseases, regrow limbs or revive the dead. **Those claims are unverified and medically unsupported.** They must not be presented as expected outcomes, a substitute for care, a diagnostic method, or evidence that an illness was removed. The book's assertions about seeing entities or accessing other minds are also unverified.

## What FocusBridge21 actually implements

**Route:** Quiet Deck → **Seven-Center Cleansing Imagery** → QMM → Chapter 7.

- **Ten original, non-verbatim stages:** supported arrival, the seven color/location centers in the book's exact bottom-up order, a calm settling phase, and grounded return.
- **Symbolic Release** (default): imagine a common worry getting lighter and fading as each color is pictured. This does not imply anything physical is burned away.
- **Colors Only:** imagine the colors or their written names without any “negativity” metaphor. Feeling nothing unusual counts as a perfectly valid session.
- Each center's color is **labeled with text**, not communicated only by a colored dot. Front/back descriptions are explicitly phrased as **imagined points**, not anatomical openings.
- Timed choices: **3, 5, 10, 15, 20, 30 minutes**. Silent by default; original browser-spoken prompts are opt-in for each session. Browser speech may rely on online voices depending on device.
- Existing **Pause, Resume, Return Now, safety check** and **Signal Ledger** are used. There is no required headset, binaural beat, audio oscillator, fixed breath pattern, physical movement or heat production.
- The ledger captures source chapter, sequence, colors, chosen imagery style, last stage, timer completion and optional narration, plus the usual optional written reflection. **Completing a timer means completing the selected practice, not chakra cleansing or medical improvement.**
- **QMM is a prerequisite.** A user who returns early during QMM cannot unlock Chapter 7 via the normal journey flow. A subsequent new visit starts QMM again.
- The Home card, both web entrypoints and PWA offline cache include Chapter 7's standalone guidance module.

## Safety and clear boundaries

If the imagery provokes distress, anxiety, dizziness or an uncomfortable feeling, stop, keep/open your eyes and orient to your real surroundings. You need not force sensation in the head, chest, throat, or elsewhere. Do not use this experience to treat, explain or evaluate cancer, infections, diabetes, psychiatric symptoms, heart disease or other health conditions.

The book's extraordinary assertions belong in the **source view**, while the guided experience is a **symbolic relaxation and attention exercise**. The app measures no chakra, “negativity,” aura, mental illness or paranormal phenomenon.
