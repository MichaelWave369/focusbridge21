# Chapters 4–5: Seven-Center Light and Clairaudience | FocusBridge21

## Source and faithful order

Primary source: **The Book of Knowledge** (Anonymous), user-supplied 112-page PDF:

- **Chapter 4, "Chakra Meditations," PDF pages 14–15:** the author describes a series of exercises picturing white or gold light at these centers in order: **Crown, Third Eye, Throat, Heart, Solar Plexus, Sacral, Root**. The text permits other colors, says to sit or lie down and quiet the mind, suggests repetition for one or two weeks, and interprets tingling, pressure, or heat as indicating chakra "opening." The latter interpretation is **not established science**, nor is the suggested schedule a medical requirement.
- **Chapter 5, "Clairaudience," PDF page 16:** the author explicitly begins with **Q-MM and GDM**, then directs attention to the ears (one at a time if useful), imagines energy moving outward and returning, and claims this can enable perception of spiritual sounds and voices. The claimed messages, beings or dimensions are **not verified external phenomena**.

All in-app prompts are freshly authored and do not reproduce large passages from the PDF.

## Chapter 4 implementation

**Route:** Quiet Mind (QMM) → **Seven-Center Light Meditation**.

Ten timed stages: orientation, the seven major centers in the book's stated top-down order, resting without interpretation, then a grounded return.

Each center is presented as an **imagined white/golden glow**, not a physical organ or energy diagnostic. No physical sensations or visual vividness are required, and no treatment or chakra opening is claimed. The practice does not require completing GDM or Breathing Energy, since the chapter's meditations themselves call for relaxing/quieting the mind rather than explicitly naming those two chapters.

## Chapter 5 implementation

**Route:** Quiet Mind (QMM) → Going Deeper (GDM) → **Clairaudience · Listening Imagery**.

Seven stages: orientation after QMM/GDM, attention to both ears, listening to normal ambient sounds, picturing an outward wave, picturing returning awareness, distinguishing actual sounds from imagined sounds, and a grounded return. The wave is **purely imagined**. There is no attempt to receive real messages or infer the activities of other people.

The author suggests that muffled conversations or voices might occur and interprets them spiritually. That is the author's assertion, not an outcome to pursue or a feature the app can verify. If a user experiences distressing, persistent, or commanding voices, the correct response is to stop the exercise, orient to their environment and seek appropriate support, not to treat them as directives.

## Product requirements and safety

- New Quiet Deck cards make each exercise accessible, but route to a **fresh QMM foundation** first. Chapter 5 further requires a **fresh GDM**. A completed timed session and saved reflection provide a voluntary "Continue" action.
- Each new guided session requires its own safety check, does not require headphones, and offers **3, 5, 10, 15, 20 or 30 minutes**.
- Both are **silent-first**. Optional browser narration must be opted into anew for each session and may use device-dependent networked voices. Reading stage text silently works just as well.
- The existing **Pause**, **Resume**, and **Return now** controls remain available. Ending any prerequisite early **does not unlock** the follow-on.
- Local Signal Ledger receipts store the chosen duration, last stage, whether browser voice was enabled, whether the timed practice completed and any optional user reflection. The receipts never claim paranormal abilities, chakra changes, unusual brain states or medical effects.
- The chapter-four **Human Body Atlas** remains a separate non-meditation reference. All previously existing passive listening and chapter routes remain independent.
- Both GitHub Pages entrypoints and the offline PWA shell include `docs/fb21-chapters45.js` via a new cache revision.
