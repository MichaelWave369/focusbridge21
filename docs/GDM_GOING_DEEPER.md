# Going Deeper Meditation (GDM) | FocusBridge21 Chapter 2

## Source

*The Book of Knowledge*, attributed to Anonymous, **Chapter 2: “Going Deeper Meditation and Breathing Energy”**, page 3 of the 112-page PDF (`3503332.pdf` supplied in conversation).

The author explicitly instructs practitioners to perform **Quiet-Mind Meditation (Q-MM) first**, then imagine a descent by stepping down a ladder into a dark room or riding an escalator while facing backward. The next instruction describes an imagined backward freefall and landing at a deeper level of relaxation. The author labels this a route toward Alpha and Theta. **Those are assertions in the book**, not validated measurements of a person's EEG or proof of a spiritual realm.

Primary edition link: https://www.scribd.com/doc/120678976/The-Book-Of-Knowledge

## What the app implements

GDM is a standalone, **silent-first** timed meditation profile that **cannot be entered directly without starting QMM first**. Its guided sequence is original and non-verbatim. The optional imagery choice makes the method tolerable for users who dislike falling or motion sensations.

**Route:**
1. QMM (3, 5, 10, 15, 20, or 30 minutes) with safety check, timer, optional speech, and grounded ending.
2. Voluntary Continue to GDM, with a fresh safety check. QMM may be ended early at any time, but early exit does not offer continuation.
3. GDM (3, 5, 10, 15, 20, or 30 minutes), with five phases and a fully optional imaginary motion choice.
4. Return/reflect. After finishing GDM, users may choose to continue into Threshold Explorer or their originally selected Focus Sync session. A new safety and headphones check applies to that session.

**QMM -> GDM -> chosen exploration target** is required when a user selects either Focus Sync or Threshold Explorer from the Quiet Deck home screen. Standalone QMM offers GDM next; standalone GDM (after QMM) offers Threshold Explorer next. The user is never automatically advanced.

## GDM's five phases

| Stage | Share | Training intention |
|---|---:|---|
| Supported & steady | 15% | Notice the room and physical body support, without forcing breath |
| Imagine going deeper | 25% | Imagine ladder descent, backward escalator motion, or simply rest |
| A gentle imagined landing | 15% | Optional soft backward-drop visualization; **never actually fall or move** |
| Rest without chasing | 25% | Enjoy the quiet scene without interpreting thoughts or imagery as facts |
| Return to the room | 20% | Feel hands, feet, and support; open eyes and reorient |

### Imagery modes

- **Ladder into quiet:** picture a gradual descent into a dim, comfortable room.
- **Backward escalator:** picture a gentle ride facing backward; the body stays still.
- **Stillness alternative:** no motion imagery, and no falling suggestion.

A user can change the selected imagery before starting. Spoken guidance is off by default and is opt-in **per GDM session**. Browser speech may depend on network-backed system voices.

## Boundaries and safety

- Treat the ladder, escalator and landing as **images in the mind**, never an instruction to climb, lean backward, or fall in the physical world.
- There are no audio oscillators, binaural beat dependencies, headphones or claimed neural measurements in GDM.
- “Alpha,” “Theta,” “trance,” “energy” and astral terms are the author's interpretation, **not a status the app detects**.
- If closing eyes or imagining movement causes anxiety, dizziness, nausea or disorientation, keep eyes open, switch to stillness, or use **Return now**.
- Neither QMM nor GDM is a treatment, a test of spiritual ability, or proof of physical out-of-body movement.
- The local Signal Ledger records the timed stage reached, visual selection, whether guidance was opted into, session length, intended next exercise and optional reflection. Completion means finishing a timed practice, **not mastery**.
- Passive listening, Sleep Sanctuary, Deep Focus for work, Intention Studio, comfort audio and Rife Frequency Lab remain independent. The separate legacy laboratory has not been rewritten to force QMM.
