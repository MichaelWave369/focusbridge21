## The number sequence

The fictional sequence **4, 8, 15, 16, 23, 42** sums to **108**. For this original preset, the numbers play two harmless creative roles:

| Number | Default mix level | Chapter title | Chapter minutes in full 108-minute route |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 4 | Soft pulse: 4% | Shoreline | 4 |
| 8 | Warm sub-bass: 8% | The Canopy | 8 |

| **Total** | Six independent sound layers | Six chapters | **108 minutes** |

The six chapters are atmospheric prompts on screen and **do not trigger jump scares, alarm tones, music, scripted story narration or changes to the audio playback graph**. They are not presented as paranormal signals or consciousness measurements.

The default session is exactly **6,480 seconds**. Additional session lengths of 15, 45, 60 and 180 minutes preserve the **same proportional chapter lengths** to remain useful for short or long listening. The stage index derives from elapsed time divided by session duration, rather than checking or judging a participant's mental state.

## Setup and user choice

- Enter through the homepage **Island 108** feature below the original 3×3 mode grid, or the **Try Island 108** shortcut in Listen & drift's setup. Both paths lead to the same dedicated profile.
- Original ambience has ocean, rain, wind, gentle bass and hum with a very quiet optional pulse. **Stereo binaural tones begin OFF**, so a speaker session can start with the standard comfort/safety confirmation alone.
- The six sliders, HUM note picker, optional stereo tones, and session duration are adjustable before playback. If binaural stereo tones are enabled, the existing headphone check becomes required.
- **Restore original island six-layer mix** returns all six levels, C3 HUM, tones off, and the 108-minute full route, then saves the isolated island preset mix locally.
- Each island adjustment persists in the existing per-profile storage under the **lost** key without modifying the drift, sleep, focus, sync or holistic profile values.
- Real-time on-screen chapter names change at their boundaries without audio chimes. The ending is the existing gentle return experience, with optional local Signal Ledger reflection.
- The Signal Ledger identifies the island preset, selected duration, the 4/8/15/16/23/42 listening timeline and completion or interruption. The receipt is **not** evidence of any paranormal or physiological effect.
